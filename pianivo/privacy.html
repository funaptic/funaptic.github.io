Privacy Policy for the “Pianivo” app

Last updated: November 2026

This privacy policy explains which personal data is processed when you use the Android app “Pianivo” (the “App”), for what purposes, and what rights you have. In case of doubt, the German version applies.

1. Controller

Henrik Jansen (Funaptic)
Kamperhofweg 9
45470 Mülheim an der Ruhr
Germany
Email: funaptic.apps@gmail.com

2. General

The App can be used without registration or an account. We do not operate any servers to which the App sends data. Your songs, progress and settings are stored only locally on your device. Personal data is processed only in connection with advertising served by Google AdMob (see section 5).

3. Data that stays on your device

3.1 Your songs and progress

Songs you create or import as MIDI files, as well as your results (stars), are stored in the App's private storage on your device. This data is not sent to us. It is deleted when you uninstall the App or clear its data in the Android settings. If Android Auto Backup is enabled, Android may back up this data to your Google account according to your settings and Google's privacy policy.

3.2 Microphone (“Real piano” mode)

If you enable “Real piano” mode, the App asks for permission to use the microphone. Sound is analysed in real time on your device only, to detect whether you played the right key. No recordings are stored and no audio is transmitted. The microphone is only active while this mode is on and the App is in the foreground. You can revoke the permission at any time in the Android settings.

3.3 Files and MIDI keyboards

MIDI files you import are read on your device only. A connected MIDI keyboard (USB/Bluetooth) is used only to detect the keys you play; no data is transmitted.

4. Links to external services

The App contains links to external services, e.g. YouTube videos you assign to a song, or websites offering MIDI files (e.g. BitMidi, MIDIWorld, Mutopia Project, Piano-MIDI.de). Opening such a link opens the site in your browser or the YouTube app; the respective provider is responsible for any data processing there. If you share a YouTube video to Pianivo, only the link is stored in the App.

5. Advertising with Google AdMob

The App is free and financed by advertising. We use Google AdMob, a service of Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland (“Google”). A full-screen ad may be shown before a song or lesson starts.

5.1 Data processed

  • your device's advertising ID (Android Advertising ID) or other device identifiers,
  • IP address (from which an approximate location can be derived),
  • device and app information (e.g. device model, operating system, language, app version),
  • interactions with ads (e.g. whether an ad was shown or tapped),
  • diagnostic and performance data.

5.2 Purposes

The data is used to serve ads, limit how often they are shown, measure and report on ads, and prevent fraud and abuse. With your consent, Google may also use it to show you personalised ads.

5.3 Consent and legal basis

When you first start the App, a consent dialog (Google User Messaging Platform) asks whether you agree to processing for personalised ads and to storing or accessing information on your device. In that case the legal basis is your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR, § 25(1) TDDDG). If you do not consent, only non-personalised or limited ads are shown; the processing required for this is based on our legitimate interest in financing the free App (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

You can change your choice or withdraw your consent at any time: tap “Datenschutz-Einstellungen (Werbung)” (privacy settings) at the bottom of the App's start screen. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in the Android settings.

5.4 Transfers to third countries

Google may transfer data to Google LLC in the USA. Google LLC is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework, for which the European Commission has adopted an adequacy decision (Art. 45 GDPR).

More information: Google Privacy Policy · How Google uses information from partner apps · Google Ad Settings

6. Google Play

The App is distributed via Google Play. Google processes the data required for downloads and updates (e.g. your Google account) under its own responsibility. We only receive aggregated, non-personal statistics (e.g. number of installs) and crash reports that do not identify you.

7. Contact by email

If you contact us by email, we process your email address and message to answer your request (Art. 6(1)(b) or (f) GDPR). The data is deleted once it is no longer needed and no legal retention obligations apply.

8. Your rights

  • access (Art. 15 GDPR), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction (Art. 18),
  • data portability (Art. 20), objection to processing based on legitimate interests (Art. 21),
  • withdrawal of consent with effect for the future (Art. 7(3)),
  • lodging a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority (Art. 77).

As we do not store any data about you ourselves, please also contact Google directly regarding data processed by AdMob. For any questions, contact us at funaptic.apps@gmail.com.

9. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

10. Changes

We update this policy when the App or legal requirements change. The version published here applies.
